Buying Guide

How to choose the right coffee machine for you.

Bean-to-cup: set it and forget it

Bean-to-cup machines grind whole beans and brew automatically. They're ideal if you want fresh coffee with minimal effort. You pay more upfront but save on per-cup costs compared to pods. Look for a removable brew group for easier cleaning.

Semi-automatic: for the coffee geek

If you enjoy the ritual — grinding, dosing, tamping, extracting — a semi-automatic machine like the Sage Barista Express gives you full control. The learning curve is real, but the results are café-quality once you dial it in.

Pod machines: convenience above all

Pick up a cup, pop in a capsule, press a button. Pod machines are the fastest and simplest option, but you're locked into a proprietary capsule system with ongoing costs of 30–45p per cup. Best for light users or as a second machine.

Milk systems: automatic vs manual

Automatic milk systems (like the LatteCrema carafe) froth and clean themselves — perfect for busy mornings. Manual steam wands give you more control over foam texture but require practice. If you mostly drink black coffee, a manual wand or no milk system is fine.

Counter space matters

Bean-to-cup machines are substantial. The Dinamica Plus and Barista Express need a permanent spot with clearance for the water tank and drip tray. The Nespresso Pop+ and Melitta Solo are notably more compact. Measure your worktop before buying.
