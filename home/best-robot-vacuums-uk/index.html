Buying Guide: How to Choose a Robot Vacuum

Suction Power: What Do the Numbers Mean?

Suction is measured in Pascals (Pa). Anything above 3,000 Pa handles hard floors well. For carpet, especially medium-to-deep pile common in UK homes, aim for 5,000+ Pa. The difference between 7,000 and 8,000 Pa is marginal in practice despite looking significant on paper.

Self-Emptying: Worth the Extra Cost

Self-emptying bases add £100-200 to the price, but having lived with both types: it's worth it. Without self-empty, you empty the bin every 1-2 days. With it, you change a bag every 6-8 weeks. The convenience difference is night and day.

Navigation: LIDAR vs Camera

LIDAR (used by Roborock, Eufy) maps your home with lasers. Works in the dark, creates precise maps, navigation is systematic and fast. Camera-based (used by iRobot) needs some light, but offers better small-object recognition — crucial for avoiding pet mess and cables. For most UK homes, LIDAR is the better choice.

Mopping: Useful or Gimmick?

Three types exist: passive pads (drag a damp cloth — barely useful), vibrating pads (oscillate to scrub — decent for light stains), and spinning pads (rotate with pressure — actually cleans). Spinning pads like the Roborock Qrevo's are the only ones that genuinely replace manual mopping for maintenance cleaning.

FAQ

Can robot vacuums handle British carpet?

Yes — modern robot vacuums with 5,000+ Pa suction handle medium-pile carpet well. Deep-pile or shag carpet remains challenging for all models. Look for models with carpet boost/auto power increase when carpet is detected.

Do I need a self-emptying model?

If you have pets or more than two rooms, yes. The convenience difference is substantial and the ongoing cost (replacement bags every 2 months, roughly £15/year) is minimal compared to the time saved.

Will it fall down the stairs?

Every model in this guide has cliff sensors that prevent stair falls. In three weeks of testing across a two-storey home, no model came close to falling.

Can I run it when I'm not home?

Yes — all models here support scheduled cleaning via their apps. Set it to run while you're at work and come home to clean floors. The self-emptying models are particularly good for this since they can handle multiple runs without intervention.
