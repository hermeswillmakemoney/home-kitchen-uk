Buying Guide

What to look for in a robot vacuum.

Navigation matters more than suction

A robot with 3,000Pa suction and good LiDAR mapping will clean your home better than one with 8,000Pa that bumps into walls randomly. LiDAR-based models build accurate floor plans and clean in efficient patterns. Gyro navigation (like the Hoover HG2) is cheaper but less precise — fine for small open-plan spaces, less ideal for cluttered rooms with lots of furniture legs.

Self-empty stations are worth the premium

If you can stretch your budget, a model with a self-empty station transforms the experience. Without one, you're emptying a small dustbin every two or three days — which defeats the purpose of an autonomous cleaner. The eufy L60 and X10 Pro Omni both include stations; the Shark PowerDetect uses a "never-touch" base that serves a similar function.

Anti-hair wrap is essential for longer hair

If anyone in your household has hair longer than a few centimetres — or you own a long-haired pet — prioritise models with anti-hair wrap brushrolls. The Shark models (PowerDetect and Matrix Plus) both feature this, and it genuinely saves you from the weekly ritual of cutting tangled hair out of the brush with scissors.

Mopping is a bonus, not a replacement

Every model on this list offers some form of mopping, but it's important to set realistic expectations. Robot mops handle light dust and surface-level dirt on hard floors — they won't remove dried-on spills, sticky footprints, or properly scrub a kitchen floor after a messy tea. Think of it as maintenance mopping between proper deep cleans. The eufy X10 Pro Omni's mop washing and drying is the most advanced on this list if mopping is a priority.

Runtime should match your home size

As a rough guide: a two-bed flat needs about 60–90 minutes, a three-bed semi around 90–150 minutes, and anything larger benefits from 150+ minutes or a model that can recharge and resume. The eufy X10 Pro Omni's 260-minute runtime is overkill for most homes but gives enormous headroom. The Shark Matrix Plus's 120 minutes is fine for average-sized properties.
