Shark PowerDetect NeverTouch Pro 2-in-1

£399.99 · The robot vacuum that genuinely reduces your chores, rather than adding new ones.

The PowerDetect NeverTouch Pro earns its top spot by combining genuinely clever engineering with everyday practicality. The self-cleaning brushroll means you're not fishing tangled hair out every week, and the never-touch base handles emptying the dustbin so your hands never meet the grime. With 180 minutes of runtime and 360° LiDAR mapping, it covers a three-bed semi in a single charge without missing corners.

What sets it apart from cheaper rivals is the precision home mapping — it learns your layout, respects no-go zones you draw in the app, and builds efficient cleaning routes rather than bouncing around at random. At £399.99, it sits at a sensible mid-point: far more capable than the sub-£200 options, yet not eye-wateringly expensive like the all-in-one station models.

eufy X10 Pro Omni Robot Vacuum & Mop

£599 · The all-in-one station that truly lets you forget about cleaning.

The X10 Pro Omni is the robot vacuum that comes closest to the dream: press a button once a week and never think about it again. The all-in-one station auto-empties the dustbin, washes the mop pads, and dries them with warm air — so you're not dealing with damp, smelly mop cloths. At 8,000Pa it's the most powerful model on this list, which matters for embedded dirt in carpet pile.

The 260-minute runtime is extraordinary — it could clean a small flat roughly three times on a single charge. Combined with iPath laser navigation and obstacle avoidance, it's as close to a "set and forget" experience as the current market offers. The £599 price tag is steep, but you're paying for the full autonomous experience. If you can afford it, you won't find a more hands-off robot vacuum.

eufy L60 Hybrid Robot Vacuum & Mop

£400 · Pet-friendly design with the suction power to handle shedding season.

If you share your home with a dog or cat, the L60 Hybrid is purpose-built for the challenge. The 5,000Pa suction is strong enough to pull embedded pet hair from carpet fibres, while the auto-lifting mop means it won't smear muddy paw prints across your hard floors when transitioning between rooms. eufy explicitly markets this as pet-friendly, and the 122 reviews back it up with a solid 4.6-star average.

The self-empty station is a particular boon for pet owners — without it, you'd be emptying a dustbin full of fur every other day. The 180-minute runtime means it can handle a full clean of a pet-filled home without interruption, and the iPath laser navigation with multi-floor mapping lets it cope with stairs (you carry it up and it remembers the upstairs layout). At £400, it's competitively priced for a self-empty model.

The Matrix Clean grid navigation — Shark's methodical back-and-forth pattern — ensures thorough coverage rather than the random bouncing you get from cheaper robots. The anti-hair wrap technology on the brushroll is a Shark signature that genuinely works, especially for households with longer-haired residents.

At £199.99, this is the cheapest 2-in-1 model on our list that still delivers on both functions. The 120-minute runtime is sufficient for most average-sized homes, and the precision LiDAR home mapping means it builds a reliable floor plan. If you have mostly hard floors and want a robot that handles both vacuuming and damp-mopping without breaking the bank, this is your pick.

Hoover HG2 Turbo Slim Hydro Robot Vacuum

£189.99 · A surprisingly capable robot at a price that won't hurt your wallet.

Don't let the "budget" label fool you — the HG2 Turbo Slim Hydro punches well above its weight. At just 7.5cm tall, it slips under sofas, beds, and sideboards that bulkier robots simply can't reach. The turbo suction boost kicks in automatically on carpets, and the vacuum-and-mop combination handles everyday dust, crumbs, and light spills with ease. The 150-minute runtime is respectable and covers a typical UK terraced house comfortably.

It's worth noting the sample size — just 19 reviews — so the 4.7-star average should be taken with a pinch of salt. But the reviews that exist are genuinely enthusiastic, and the spec sheet at this price is hard to argue with. If you're curious about robot vacuums but hesitant to drop £400+, the Hoover HG2 is a low-risk way to find out whether a robot cleaner suits your lifestyle.
