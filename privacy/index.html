Privacy Policy

How we handle your data. Simple and transparent.

Who We Are

Home & Kitchen UK is a review website operated from the United Kingdom.

What Data We Collect

We use Plausible Analytics, a privacy-friendly analytics tool that does not use cookies and does not collect personal data. It tells us which pages are popular and how people find our site — nothing more.

We do not:

  • Use tracking cookies
  • Collect email addresses (unless you contact us)
  • Create user accounts
  • Sell or share data with third parties

Affiliate Links

When you click an affiliate link to Amazon or another retailer, the retailer may place a cookie on your device to track the referral. This is standard affiliate marketing practice. These cookies are set by the retailer, not by us.
