Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. Here's exactly what data we collect (spoiler: none), what cookies we use (none), and your rights under GDPR.

Our commitment: Home Kitchen UK does not collect, store, or process any personal data from visitors. We do not use tracking cookies, analytics scripts, or fingerprinting technologies. You can browse our site with complete privacy.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information from visitors to this website. Specifically, we do not:

  • Collect names, email addresses, or contact details
  • Use analytics tools (Google Analytics, Plausible, etc.) to track your behaviour
  • Set tracking cookies or use any form of browser fingerprinting
  • Store IP addresses or device information
  • Use social media tracking pixels (Facebook Pixel, Twitter Pixel, etc.)
  • Employ retargeting or remarketing technologies

Our website is a static site served through Netlify. Your web browser and Netlify's servers may log standard HTTP requests (including your IP address) for operational purposes such as DDoS protection and uptime monitoring, but we have no access to or control over these logs.

2. Cookies

This website does not set any cookies. You will not receive any cookie consent banners from us because there are no cookies to consent to.

When you click on an affiliate link and are redirected to a retailer's website (such as eBay, Argos, or Currys), that retailer may set its own cookies on your device. These cookies are governed by the retailer's own privacy policy, not ours. We have no control over and accept no responsibility for the cookies set by third-party websites.

Note on affiliate links: Our affiliate links (via eBay Partner Network, Skimlinks, and other programmes) may contain tracking parameters that allow our affiliate partners to attribute a sale to our referral. These parameters are part of the URL and are transmitted only to the retailer when you click the link. They do not install cookies or collect data from our site.

3. Affiliate Links & Third-Party Sites

As disclosed in our Affiliate Disclosure, we participate in affiliate programmes. When you click on a product link on our site:

  • You are redirected to the retailer's website via an affiliate tracking link.
  • The affiliate network (e.g., eBay Partner Network, Skimlinks) may set a cookie on your device to track the referral for commission attribution purposes.
  • This cookie is set by the third-party network, not by Home Kitchen UK.
  • The cookie typically expires within 30–90 days, depending on the programme.

We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of the retailers and affiliate networks you interact with for full details on their data practices.

4. GDPR Compliance

Under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018, because we do not collect or process any personal data, most data subject rights are not applicable to our site. However, we respect your rights and are transparent about our practices.

Right to Access

Since we do not collect any personal data, there is no personal data to provide access to. If you believe we hold data about you, contact us and we will investigate.

Right to Rectification

We do not store personal data, so there is no data to rectify.

Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten")

We do not store personal data, so there is no data to erase. Any server logs are managed by our hosting provider (Netlify) and are outside our control.

Right to Restrict Processing

We do not process personal data, so this right is not applicable.

Right to Data Portability

As we do not collect personal data, there is no data to port.

Right to Object

We do not process personal data for direct marketing or any other purpose, so there is nothing to object to.

5. Children's Privacy

Our website is a general-audience site. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 16. Since we do not collect any personal data from any visitors, this is inherently satisfied.

6. External Links

Our site contains links to external websites operated by third parties (retailers, manufacturers, and other sites). We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of these external sites. We encourage you to read the privacy policy of every website you visit.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
