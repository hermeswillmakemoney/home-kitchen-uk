About Home & Kitchen UK

We test home and kitchen appliances in real British homes so you can buy with confidence.

Our Mission

Buying a new appliance — whether it's an air fryer, a robot vacuum, or a coffee machine — should be exciting, not stressful. But with hundreds of options, confusing specifications, and reviews you can't trust, it often feels like a gamble.

Home & Kitchen UK exists to fix that. We test products in real UK homes, on UK mains voltage, with British ingredients and British standards in mind. Every recommendation we make is one we'd give to a friend.

How We Test

We don't test in labs. Every product is used in a real home kitchen or living space for a minimum of two weeks. We cook real meals, clean real messes, and live with these products the way you would.

We measure:

  • Performance: Does it do what it claims? Consistently?
  • Ease of use: Is it intuitive or frustrating?
  • Build quality: Will it last beyond the warranty period?
  • Value: Is it worth the money compared to alternatives?
  • UK relevance: Does it work well with UK power, UK-sized spaces, and UK-available consumables?
