How to Choose the Right Air Fryer

Whether you're a first-time buyer or looking to upgrade, understanding the key factors will help you make a confident choice. Here's what to consider before buying.

Capacity: How Big Do You Need?

Capacity is the single most important factor in choosing an air fryer. Get it wrong and you'll either be cooking in batches (too small) or wasting energy heating empty space (too large).

  • 1-2 people: 3-4 litres is ideal. The Tefal Easy Fry & Grill 4.2L or Ninja 3.8L are perfect for singles and couples.
  • 3-4 people: Aim for 5-7 litres. The Bosch Series 6 7.2L is our pick for families of this size.
  • 5+ people or meal-prepping: Go for 8+ litres. The Tower T17088 11L and Ninja Foodi MAX 9.5L are built for big batches.

Dual Basket vs Single Basket

Dual-basket air fryers let you cook two different dishes at different temperatures simultaneously. This is genuinely transformative for weeknight dinners: protein in one basket, veg in the other, both finished at the same time. However, they cost more and take up significantly more counter space. If you're cooking for one or two people, a single basket is usually plenty.

Cooking Functions: Which Do You Actually Need?

Most air fryers offer a core set of functions, and premium models add extras. Here's what they all mean:

  • Air Fry: The core function. Rapid hot air circulation cooks food with minimal oil.
  • Roast: Lower temperature, longer cooking — ideal for vegetables and meats.
  • Bake: For cakes, muffins, and breads. Works surprisingly well in most models.
  • Grill: Top-down heat for browning and char. Not all models have this.
  • Reheat: Revives leftovers far better than a microwave.
  • Dehydrate: Low, slow heat for dried fruits, jerky, and herbs.
  • Max Crisp: Ninja's high-temperature blast for frozen foods.

Power and Energy Efficiency

Air fryers are generally more energy-efficient than conventional ovens because they're smaller and cook faster. A 2400W air fryer cooking for 20 minutes uses roughly the same energy as a 3000W oven running for 40 minutes, but the food is done in half the time. In practice, switching from oven to air fryer can save 20-30% on cooking energy costs.

Warranty and Aftercare

Most air fryers come with a one-year warranty as standard. The Bosch Series 6 stands out with a two-year warranty, which reflects the brand's confidence in the product's longevity. Look for dishwasher-safe parts, as they make cleaning significantly easier — all five models in our roundup have them.
