How We Rank

Our methodology

We don't accept free products or paid placements. Every ranking is built from data — not opinions.

Collect Reviews

We aggregate verified customer reviews from Amazon UK, Argos, Currys, John Lewis, and retailer direct sites. No fake or incentivised reviews make it through.

Analyse Patterns

We score products on build quality, performance, value, and common failure points. Recurring praise and complaints carry more weight than one-off opinions.

Compare & Rank

Products in the same category are scored against each other on a consistent rubric. We factor in price-to-performance ratios to surface genuine value leaders.

Publish & Update

Rankings are updated monthly with fresh review data and price checks. If a product's consensus shifts, its ranking moves — we don't lock in winners.
