Buying Guide

What to look for in a cordless vacuum.

Suction power matters — but it's not everything

Dyson models publish their suction in Air Watts (AW), with the V15 leading at 240AW. Shark and Numatic don't publish comparable figures, but that doesn't mean their vacuums underperform. Brushroll design, airflow engineering, and floor head quality all affect real-world cleaning. A well-designed 115AW vacuum can outperform a poorly engineered 200AW one on certain surfaces. Focus on how a vacuum cleans in reviews rather than raw numbers alone.

Match runtime to your home size

As a rough guide: a one- or two-bed flat needs about 20–30 minutes, a three-bed semi around 40–60 minutes, and anything larger benefits from the full 60-minute models. The Shark PowerPro and Dyson V15 both offer 60 minutes, making them suitable for larger homes. The Hetty Quick's 30 minutes is fine for smaller spaces but will leave you hunting for a charger in a four-bed detached.

Anti-hair wrap is essential for pet owners and longer hair

If you have pets or anyone in the household has hair longer than shoulder-length, prioritise a vacuum with anti-hair wrap technology. Both Shark models feature this, and the Dyson V8 Absolute Pet uses a tangle-free turbine tool. Without it, you'll be cutting tangled hair out of the brushbar with scissors every few weeks — and nobody wants that chore.

Weight affects everyday usability more than you'd think

The difference between 2.5kg (Henry Hetty Quick) and 3.3kg (Shark Anti Hair Wrap) doesn't sound huge on paper, but it's very noticeable when you're vacuuming stairs or holding the vacuum above head level for curtains and skirting boards. If you're buying for an older person, someone with limited mobility, or you simply value ease of use, the lighter models make a real difference in day-to-day comfort.

HEPA filtration is worth seeking out for allergy sufferers

The Dyson V15's whole-machine HEPA filtration captures 99.97% of particles down to 0.3 microns — pollen, dust mites, pet dander, and mould spores. The V8 Absolute Pet also features whole-machine filtration, though not HEPA-grade. If anyone in your home has allergies or asthma, this is one spec worth paying attention to. The Henry Hetty Quick's washable filters also help maintain air quality, though they're not HEPA-rated.
