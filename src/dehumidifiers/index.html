How to Choose the Right Dehumidifier

Whether you're tackling condensation for the first time or upgrading an older unit, understanding the key specifications will help you pick a dehumidifier that actually solves your problem. Here's what to consider before buying.

Extraction Rate: How Much Moisture Do You Need to Remove?

The extraction rate, measured in litres per day, tells you how much water the dehumidifier can pull from the air in 24 hours. This is the single most important specification to match to your situation.

  • 3-6L per day: Suitable for small single rooms up to 15m² — bathrooms, box bedrooms, or airing cupboards. The Bush DH-CSK03 3L fits here.
  • 10-12L per day: The sweet spot for most homes. Covers living rooms, kitchens, and bedrooms up to 35-40m². The Meaco Arete One 12L and Ecostrure PureDry 12L are ideal choices.
  • 20L+ per day: For large open-plan spaces, basements, or whole-floor drying. The Princess Smart 20L and Meaco Arete One 25L are built for these situations.

Compressor vs Desiccant: Which Technology?

All five models in our roundup use compressor technology, which is the most common and energy-efficient type for standard home use. Compressor dehumidifiers work best in rooms above 15°C, making them ideal for heated living spaces throughout most of the year.

Desiccant dehumidifiers use a different mechanism (a moisture-absorbing material) and work better in colder environments like unheated garages or conservatories in winter. However, they consume significantly more electricity — often 600-800W compared to 180-400W for compressor models. Unless you specifically need cold-room performance, a compressor model will save you money on running costs.

Tank Size and Continuous Drainage

The water tank capacity determines how often you need to empty the unit. A 12L dehumidifier with a 2-litre tank running at full capacity in damp conditions could fill up every 4 hours. In normal use, once or twice a day is more typical.

If you want a set-and-forget solution, look for a model with a continuous drainage option. You attach a standard hose to the outlet and the unit pumps water directly into a drain or sink. The Meaco Arete One 25L supports this, making it ideal for basements, utility rooms, or anywhere you don't want to think about emptying a tank.

Noise Levels: Where Will You Use It?

Dehumidifier noise matters more than you might think, especially if you plan to run one in a bedroom or home office. Here's a rough guide:

  • 35-38dB: Whisper quiet. The Bush DH-CSK03 achieves this thanks to its Peltier technology, though it sacrifices extraction capacity.
  • 40-44dB: Quiet — like a quiet conversation. The Meaco Arete One 12L (41dB) and Ecostrure PureDry 12L (42dB) sit here. Fine for bedrooms on the low setting.
  • 45dB+: Noticeable — like a refrigerator hum. The Meaco 25L (46dB) is in this bracket. Fine for living areas but too loud for sleeping.

Running Costs and Energy Efficiency

Dehumidifier running costs are a legitimate concern given current UK energy prices. Here's a practical breakdown:

  • Bush DH-CSK03 (40W): Roughly 1p per hour, or 24p per day running continuously. Virtually negligible.
  • Meaco Arete One 12L (186W): About 5p per hour at full power. However, the humidistat means it cycles off once the target humidity is reached, so actual daily cost is typically 50-80p.
  • Meaco Arete One 25L (374W): Around 10p per hour at full power, but again, intelligent cycling reduces this in practice to roughly £1-1.50 per day.

Compare this to the cost of mould remediation, damaged furniture, or health issues from prolonged exposure to damp air, and a dehumidifier quickly pays for itself.

Extra Features Worth Considering

Beyond the core specs, these features add genuine value:

  • Laundry mode: Boosts extraction to dry washing hung indoors. Invaluable during autumn and winter. All five models in our roundup include this.
  • Humidistat: Lets you set a target humidity level (40-60% is ideal). The unit then maintains it automatically, saving energy.
  • Air purifier: The Meaco Arete One 12L and Bush DH-CSK03 include HEPA filtration, removing dust, pollen, and allergens as they dehumidify.
  • Smart/app control: The Princess Smart 20L offers WiFi connectivity for remote monitoring and control from your phone.
  • Timer: Programme the unit to run during off-peak hours for cheaper electricity. Available on most models.
  • Auto defrost: Prevents the compressor coil from icing up in cooler temperatures. The Ecostrure PureDry 12L includes this.
