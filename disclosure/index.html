Affiliate Disclosure

How we earn money, which programmes we participate in, and what that means for you as a reader.

Affiliate Disclosure: Home Kitchen UK is a participant in various affiliate advertising programmes. This means we earn commissions when you click on our links and make qualifying purchases. This comes at no additional cost to you.

How We Make Money

Home Kitchen UK is a free resource for UK consumers looking for honest, thorough reviews of home and kitchen products. To keep this site running and our content free, we participate in affiliate programmes. When you click on a product link on our site and make a purchase through a participating retailer, we may earn a small commission.

This does not affect the price you pay. The commission is paid by the retailer, not by you. In fact, we often negotiate or find the best available prices for the products we recommend.

Affiliate Programmes We Use

We currently participate in the following affiliate programmes:

eBay Partner Network

We earn commissions on qualifying purchases made through our eBay affiliate links. This includes new and refurbished products from verified eBay sellers across the UK.

Skimlinks

Skimlinks automatically converts standard retailer links on our site into affiliate links. This programme gives us access to commissions from hundreds of UK and international retailers including Argos, Currys, John Lewis, and many more.

Other Affiliate Networks

From time to time, we may also participate in direct affiliate programmes with individual retailers. Any such partnerships are disclosed within the relevant product pages.

How Affiliate Links Work

When you click on a product link on our site, you may be redirected to the retailer's website via an affiliate tracking link. This link contains a unique identifier that tells the retailer you came from Home Kitchen UK. If you then make a purchase, the retailer pays us a small commission as a thank-you for the referral.

These tracking links are standard across the affiliate marketing industry and are used solely to attribute the sale to our site. They do not collect personal information about you, nor do they affect the price you pay for any product.

Our Editorial Independence

We want to be completely transparent: affiliate commissions do not influence our reviews, ratings, or rankings.

  • We do not accept free products from manufacturers in exchange for reviews.
  • We do not accept payment for favourable coverage or higher rankings.
  • We do not allow affiliate partners to review or approve our content before publication.
  • Our product rankings are based on our analysis of verified customer reviews, specifications, and value for money.

Our only incentive is to recommend the best products for UK consumers, because that's what keeps readers coming back. A recommendation we don't stand behind would ultimately hurt our reputation far more than any single commission is worth.

FTC & ASA Compliance

We comply with the advertising and endorsement guidelines set out by:

  • the UK Advertising Standards Authority (ASA) — we clearly disclose our commercial relationships so readers can make informed decisions.
  • the UK Competition and Markets Authority (CMA) — we ensure all affiliate links and commercial content are clearly labelled.
  • the US Federal Trade Commission (FTC) — as an internationally accessible site, we also follow FTC guidelines on affiliate disclosure.

Prices & Availability

Prices shown on our site are correct at the time of writing but may change without notice. We make every effort to keep pricing information up to date, but we recommend checking the retailer's website for the most current pricing and availability before making a purchase.
